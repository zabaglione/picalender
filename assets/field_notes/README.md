> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: wide landscape illustration used in a 420 by 240 pixel panel of a Raspberry Pi clock, calendar, weather and moon dashboard. Generate at 1536 by 864 pixels, landscape.
>
> Primary request / style: independent 2D game illustration, stylized simplified characters, hand-drawn outlines, limited atmospheric palette, subtle texture, distinctive imperfect shapes, restrained detail, handcrafted visual identity.
>
> Scene: a quiet small forest clearing, one small relaxed rust-orange fox curled beside a few oversized simplified leaves and soft rolling hills. A calm friendly companion for an always-on calendar. Slightly irregular ink contours, flat gouache shapes and subtle paper grain, a sophisticated indie game storybook feeling. Minimal detail, strong clean silhouettes readable when small. A gently asymmetrical composition, fox near lower middle, forest silhouettes frame both sides, generous open pale sky in the upper third.
>
> Palette: warm oatmeal paper #EDE8D8, deep spruce ink #273F39, muted sage #7E917A, muted ochre #C48B54 and a small rust accent #B86343. Restrained five-color impression.
>
> Constraints: no text, letters, numbers, logos, interface elements, frame, moon, sun, stars or clock. No gradients, shiny 3D surfaces or photorealism. Fill the complete rectangle with the illustration. This is the final art asset, not a mockup.
